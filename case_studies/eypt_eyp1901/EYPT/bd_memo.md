**Model P(approval):** 71.9%

**Our edge:** The market treats EYP-1901 as a me-too anti-VEGF and discounts the ACUITY readout on Phase 3 execution risk. Three factors are underweighted: (1) real-world adherence data shows ~40% of wet AMD patients are undertreated on monthly/bimonthly schedules — semi-annual dosing is the structural fix to this problem; (2) Phase 2 DAVIO 2 showed clear non-inferiority, making ACUITY a confirmation rather than a discovery trial; (3) the competitive moat of the Durasert technology is underappreciated — no other approved therapy offers intravitreal semi-annual dosing in wet AMD.


### Kill Criteria
- ACUITY primary endpoint failure: EYP-1901 arm inferior by >4 ETDRS letters to aflibercept at week 52
- Delivery failure: <50% of patients maintain 6-month dosing interval (validates Susvimo concerns about durability)
- Safety signal: choroidal or retinal atrophy signal at rates materially above aflibercept comparator
- Payer rejection of semi-annual premium pricing (≥2× per-injection vs. Eylea) — limits uptake to cash-pay patients

## Appendix B: What Would Change This Thesis


1. ACUITY week-52 topline: BCVA NI demonstrated with ≥65% of patients at 6-month dosing → validates delivery + efficacy simultaneously (strong bull case).
2. ACUITY fails NI: primary endpoint missed → thesis ends; stock likely falls 70-80%.
3. FDA grants priority review for NDA → signals 'breakthrough-like' unmet need recognition; re-rates near-term.
4. Payer framework announcement pre-launch: if a major PBM pre-approves formulary positioning at semi-annual dosing premium → accelerates adoption trajectory.
5. Susvimo (Genentech) reports strong real-world 6-month durability data → validates semi-annual dosing as category; pulls EYP-1901 along.
6. Partnership or acquisition by Novartis/Roche/AbbVie → likely at significant premium to NAV given platform value of Durasert technology.

## Appendix D: Methodology

**rNPV:** Probability-weighted DCF. Trial costs discounted at phase midpoint, weighted by P(reaching each phase). Revenue modeled as EBIT (net revenue − COGS − SG&A), discounted at 12% WACC over a 12-year commercial horizon.

**POS model:** Industry-prior phase transition rates (Biomedtracker/IQVIA 2021, by therapeutic area) adjusted in log-odds space for: endpoint type, MoA precedent, sample adequacy, safety profile, biomarker selection, and competitive pressure. Each adjuster shifts P by ±10–25pp around the base rate.

**Monte Carlo (10000 simulations):** Phase success ~ Beta(α, β) from analyst mean + ESS; peak sales ~ log-normal (CV=81%); discount rate ~ Normal(μ, 2pp); years-to-peak ~ Normal(μ, 1.5 yrs). Correlated draws via Gaussian copula (peak_sales ↔ penetration ρ = −0.20).

**Scenario analysis:** Bull/base/bear applied as multiplicative shocks to peak sales (×1.5/1.0/0.55), POS (×1.3/1.0/0.70), duration (−0.5/0/+1.0 yrs), and WACC (−1pp/0/+2pp).
