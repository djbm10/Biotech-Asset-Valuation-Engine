**Market-implied P(approval):** 82.7% (back-solved: [market cap − net cash + weighted trial costs] ÷ gross revenue PV)

**Thesis:** The market treats EYP-1901 as a me-too anti-VEGF and discounts the ACUITY readout on Phase 3 execution risk. Three factors are underweighted: (1) real-world adherence data shows ~40% of wet AMD patients are undertreated on monthly/bimonthly schedules — semi-annual dosing is the structural fix to this problem; (2) Phase 2 DAVIO 2 showed clear non-inferiority, making ACUITY a confirmation rather than a discovery trial; (3) the competitive moat of the Durasert technology is underappreciated — no other approved therapy offers intravitreal semi-annual dosing in wet AMD.


**Kill criteria:**
- ACUITY primary endpoint failure: EYP-1901 arm inferior by >4 ETDRS letters to aflibercept at week 52
- Delivery failure: <50% of patients maintain 6-month dosing interval (validates Susvimo concerns about durability)
- Safety signal: choroidal or retinal atrophy signal at rates materially above aflibercept comparator
- Payer rejection of semi-annual premium pricing (≥2× per-injection vs. Eylea) — limits uptake to cash-pay patients
