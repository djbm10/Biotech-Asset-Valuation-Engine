**Our edge:** The market underestimates three factors: (1) inavolisib's FDA approval de-risks the regulatory path for PI3Ka-selective agents — the class is now validated; (2) RLY-2608's 300x H1047R selectivity should enable continuous dosing and CDK4/6 combinations that inavolisib cannot access; (3) H1047R accounts for ~40% of PIK3CA mutations but is underserved by current options — RLY-2608 could become class leader in this subgroup.


### Kill Criteria
- Phase 2 confirmed ORR < 30% in H1047R + fulvestrant cohort (inavolisib showed ~36% ORR in all-comers; <30% removes differentiation claim)
- Grade 3+ hyperglycemia rate > 20% (would eliminate the tolerability advantage vs. alpelisib that underlies the entire thesis)
- FDA requires OS endpoint (not ORR/PFS) for approval in 2nd-line mBC (raises bar substantially)
- Head-to-head Phase 3 vs. inavolisib fails to show superiority — regulatory approval becomes uncertain

## Appendix D: Methodology

**rNPV:** Probability-weighted DCF. Trial costs discounted at phase midpoint, weighted by P(reaching each phase). Revenue modeled as EBIT (net revenue − COGS − SG&A), discounted at 10% WACC over a 12-year commercial horizon.

**POS model:** Industry-prior phase transition rates (Biomedtracker/IQVIA 2021, by therapeutic area) adjusted in log-odds space for: endpoint type, MoA precedent, sample adequacy, safety profile, biomarker selection, and competitive pressure. Each adjuster shifts P by ±10–25pp around the base rate.

**Monte Carlo (10000 simulations):** Phase success ~ Beta(α, β) from analyst mean + ESS; peak sales ~ log-normal (CV=194%); discount rate ~ Normal(μ, 2pp); years-to-peak ~ Normal(μ, 1.5 yrs). Correlated draws via Gaussian copula (peak_sales ↔ penetration ρ = −0.20).

**Scenario analysis:** Bull/base/bear applied as multiplicative shocks to peak sales (×1.5/1.0/0.55), POS (×1.3/1.0/0.70), duration (−0.5/0/+1.0 yrs), and WACC (−1pp/0/+2pp).
