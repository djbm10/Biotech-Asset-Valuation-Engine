**Our edge:** The market is applying generic CNS Phase 3 base rates (~30%) to a program with unusually strong Phase 2 signal (p<0.001, -22pp treatment difference on a well-validated endpoint). Three factors are underweighted: (1) ET is a mechanistically clear target — thalamocortical oscillations are measurable and reproducible, unlike CNS diseases with heterogeneous pathophysiology; (2) the 75% untreated moderate-severe population represents a latent demand shock that no financial model is correctly sizing — when a well-tolerated drug exists, adherence-driven market expansion is not captured by simple penetration assumptions; (3) Praxis has a second bite at the apple (PRAX-628 for epilepsy) that is unpriced in the stock.


### Kill Criteria
- ESSENTIAL primary endpoint miss: TETRAS-ADL change from baseline fails superiority vs. placebo at week 12

- Cash crisis before ESSENTIAL readout without equity raise at premium to current price
- PRAX-628 Phase 2 failure (platform risk): reduces Praxis to single-asset company and impairs financing optionality

## Appendix D: Methodology

**rNPV:** Probability-weighted DCF. Trial costs discounted at phase midpoint, weighted by P(reaching each phase). Revenue modeled as EBIT (net revenue − COGS − SG&A), discounted at 12% WACC over a 12-year commercial horizon.

**POS model:** Industry-prior phase transition rates (Biomedtracker/IQVIA 2021, by therapeutic area) adjusted in log-odds space for: endpoint type, MoA precedent, sample adequacy, safety profile, biomarker selection, and competitive pressure. Each adjuster shifts P by ±10–25pp around the base rate.

**Monte Carlo (10000 simulations):** Phase success ~ Beta(α, β) from analyst mean + ESS; peak sales ~ log-normal (CV=78%); discount rate ~ Normal(μ, 2pp); years-to-peak ~ Normal(μ, 1.5 yrs). Correlated draws via Gaussian copula (peak_sales ↔ penetration ρ = −0.20).

**Scenario analysis:** Bull/base/bear applied as multiplicative shocks to peak sales (×1.5/1.0/0.55), POS (×1.3/1.0/0.70), duration (−0.5/0/+1.0 yrs), and WACC (−1pp/0/+2pp).
