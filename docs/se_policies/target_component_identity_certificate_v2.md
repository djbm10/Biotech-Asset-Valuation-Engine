## Scope and granularity

A certificate is scoped to its exact component/assertion and proposed identity. It is never transferable to other aliases in the same record or ingestion union. Gene, protein, and aggregate target are distinct identity levels. HGNC/UniProt cross-references express encoding, not gene/protein equivalence. No sequence comparison, normalized-name equivalence or substring reasoning.

All 3,534 original assertion origins remain available, including Greek and normalized collisions. The 48 groups retain every member; no record elected away. Existing populations are not relabelled.

Expected classes are committed before logic: 55 CERTIFIED_SINGLE_ENTITY, 35 CONFLICT, 126 AMBIGUOUS, 15 CERTIFIED_RELATIONAL_ENTITY, three CERTIFIED_MULTI_ENTITY, zero UNRESOLVED. Clean positives concern explicitly scoped primary gene or protein assertions, not alias inventories. Structural controls certify structure, never constituent equivalence.

## Deterministic rules, ordered within scope

1. PROPOSAL: resolve its exact matching sidecar assertions to their recorded resolver components with the proposed canonical ID. For approvedName/approvedSymbol assertions, a stable source gene ID differing from the explicitly elected canonical gene ID is CONFLICT. Preserve both claims even if a protein product could be shared: this is a gene-level conflict, not proof of protein non-equivalence. Otherwise merged groups with multiple distinct source gene IDs, synonyms lacking a typed entity equivalence, or incomplete lineage are AMBIGUOUS. No name-based reconciliation. All contributing assertions and supporting HGNC/UniProt/ChEMBL fields remain visible. The proposal evaluation must not read old diagnostic_status or expected labels.
2. RESOLVER_GROUP: an ingestion union is not an authority biological entity. No blanket all-alias certificate exists. AMBIGUOUS, with per-member gene claims/conflicts exposed. An individual primary assertion may independently qualify under rule 3; this does not certify the group.
3. PRIMARY_ASSERTION: only exact approvedSymbol/approvedName fields. Require exactly one source gene in the resolver component, one canonical-ID component, exact elected source gene ID, and an Approved HGNC record with the same Ensembl ID and exact approved symbol matching the proposed gene identifier. These are identifier equalities anchored by structured fields, not spelling-based alias inference. Original assertion must be the complete approved field from that gene. CERTIFIED_SINGLE_ENTITY at GENE level if all premises hold; otherwise AMBIGUOUS. No protein equivalence implied. Synonyms do not inherit this permission.
4. STRUCTURED_TARGET: explicit ChEMBL target_type and nonempty complete typed component membership required. CHIMERIC PROTEIN, PROTEIN COMPLEX, PROTEIN COMPLEX GROUP, PROTEIN FAMILY and PROTEIN-PROTEIN INTERACTION produce CERTIFIED_RELATIONAL_ENTITY; SELECTIVITY GROUP produces CERTIFIED_MULTI_ENTITY. Require constituent identifiers, not punctuation. A SINGLE PROTEIN target alone is AMBIGUOUS for equivalence because target co-membership does not certify a separate proposed identity. Unknown types abstain, missing record UNRESOLVED. Preserve every constituent, including RNA and accession absence.
5. PROTEIN_COMPONENT: exact target-to-component edge, target_type SINGLE PROTEIN, exactly one PROTEIN component, human taxon, exact accession equal to proposed UNIPROT ID, reviewed active UniProt primary accession, human taxon, and exactly one Approved HGNC gene encoding that accession with reciprocal UniProt HGNC cross-reference. Require all gene claims to agree; multiple HGNC IDs or missing/review-status evidence abstain. This certifies the PROTEIN accession only. It never equates protein to HGNC gene or approves legacy TARGET mappings.
6. No evidence produces UNRESOLVED. Available evidence failing a positive requirement produces AMBIGUOUS. No numeric confidence scores. Output includes scope, raw text, canonical identity, typed entities/constituents, structured claim/agreement matrix, exact source record and field assertion IDs, rule and rationale.

Case/certificate IDs are canonical JSON SHA-256; sorted serialization, gzip mtime zero, no timestamps/random IDs.
