# Idea 20 — Corpus Expansion to N=30 and the Tolerability Blind Spot (2026-07-03)

## What this step was

Step 2 of the Idea 20 path is growing the labeled corpus. Using the seed
worksheet (`bve.analysis.killer_question_label_worksheet`), 15 failed programs
were reviewed against their trial history and promoted to `clean` labels,
taking the headline corpus from **N=15 to N=30**.

Promotions are recorded with honest provenance
(`label_source = "phase_transitions.csv seed + Claude domain review 2026-07-03
(not primary-source adjudicated)"`) so the enlarged corpus is not mistaken for
primary-source-verified ground truth. 10 seed candidates were **rejected**
(molecule duplicates of existing rows: taselisib/ganetespib/trebananib variants)
or **held** (heuristic misfires such as BMS-936559 anti-PD-L1, whose failure was
differentiation vs. pembrolizumab, not target invalidity).

## Headline result (VOI mode, N=30)

| Metric | N=15 | N=30 | 
|--------|------|------|
| M1 top-1 | 53.3% | 66.7% |
| M1 top-2 | 93.3% | 96.7% |
| M3 (VOI) | 66.7% | 83.3% |
| M3 (openness-only) | 53.3% | **30.0%** |

Two things stand out, and neither is "the model got better":

1. **M1's rise to 66.7% is corpus composition, not skill.** The 15 promotions
   are 12 TARGET_VALIDITY + 2 TOLERABILITY + 1 DIFFERENTIATION, and the model
   handles TARGET_VALIDITY perfectly. The per-archetype slice makes this explicit:

   | Archetype | N | M1 hits | M3 correct |
   |-----------|---|---------|------------|
   | TARGET_VALIDITY | 18 | **18/18** | 18/18 |
   | TOLERABILITY_CEILING | 8 | **0/8** | 5/8 |
   | DIFFERENTIATION | 4 | 2/4 | 2/4 |

2. **The openness-only vs VOI gap on M3 blew open (30.0% vs 83.3%).** The new
   TARGET-vs-DIFFERENTIATION programs are exactly the openness ties that
   spuriously abstain; VOI resolves them. At N=15 the VOI M3 lift was +13.3, at
   N=30 it is +53.3. Growing the corpus strengthened the case for VOI far more
   than the N=15 evidence could.

## The real finding: tolerability is a systematic blind spot

TOLERABILITY_CEILING is now **0-for-8 on M1 top-1**. This is not noise; it is
structural. A non-severe safety/tolerability question enters at openness 0.5 and
shares the earliest-gate rNPV swing with every other next-gate archetype, so its
VOI is capped at 0.50 and it loses top-1 to any fully-open competitor every time.
No amount of corpus growth fixes this — it is an openness-model property.

This lines up with the path doc's "honest ceiling": DIFFERENTIATION and
TOLERABILITY_CEILING were always the archetypes most likely to stay display-only.
The N=30 data turns that expectation into a measured fact. Two ways forward, both
out of the measurement harness:

- **Accept it:** tolerability stays a display-only diligence flag, never a
  POS-influencing decisive question. Consistent with the path doc's end-state.
- **Re-weight it:** raise the openness a non-severe-but-decisive tolerability
  question enters at (a change in `killer_question.py`'s `_tolerability_ceiling`
  / `_OPENNESS`), which is a core-engine change to be justified on its own, not a
  harness tweak.

A corpus-independent regression test now pins both facts (VOI resolves
TARGET-vs-DIFFERENTIATION ties; tolerability never wins top-1) so a future fix is
visible.

## Caveats

- Labels are seed + general-knowledge review, **not primary-source adjudicated**.
  The M1/M3 numbers should be read as directional until a domain pass confirms
  the promoted rows.
- The corpus is now TARGET_VALIDITY-heavy (18/30). A balanced corpus — and
  especially the still-missing DOSE_ADEQUACY / DELIVERY_EXPOSURE archetypes,
  which `phase_transitions.csv` cannot supply — matters more for a trustworthy
  headline than raw N.
