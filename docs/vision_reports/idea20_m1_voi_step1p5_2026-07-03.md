# Idea 20 — Step 1.5: Wiring the VOI Branch Valuator (2026-07-03)

## What this step was

Step 1 made M1 non-trivial by opening the competing archetypes in each
reconstructed snapshot, dropping M1 from a meaningless 100% to a real 53.3%.
But that 53.3% baseline was driven by **openness-only ranking**: with no branch
valuator every live question scores `voi = openness`, so equal-openness
questions tie and the winner is decided by two mechanical artifacts — draft
order (the candidate built first wins) and tie-triggered abstention.

Step 1.5 threads the real **`RnpvBranchValuator`** into the replay so questions
rank by rNPV value-at-stake instead of by openness alone. The goal was to make
M1 measure ranking *skill* rather than build order.

## What changed

- **`voi_stub_program()`** — a single canonical minimal `(asset, trials, market)`
  (3-phase oncology small molecule: P2 0.37 / P3 0.55 / NDA 0.87) sufficient for
  `RnpvBranchValuator` to compute rNPV swings.
- **`replay_killer_questions_with_voi()`** — mirrors the openness-only replay but
  builds the valuator from the stub and threads it into `derive_killer_questions`.
  Same no-lookahead guard.
- **`run_killer_question_backtest(..., use_voi=True)`** — selects the replay path;
  the report carries a distinct `screening_backtest_voi` mode.
- **CLI** — `--mode {openness,voi,both}` (default `both`) prints each report plus a
  side-by-side delta.
- **Tests** — swing-collapse mechanism, tie-break-without-abstain, no-lookahead on
  the VOI path, and a regression anchor pinning M1 8/15 and M3 10/15.

## Result

| Metric | Openness-only | VOI | Δ |
|--------|---------------|-----|---|
| M1 top-1 (clean, N=15) | 53.3% | **53.3%** | +0.0 |
| M1 top-2 recall | 93.3% | 93.3% | +0.0 |
| M3 correct-abstention | 53.3% | **66.7%** | **+13.3** |

## What actually moved, and why M1 didn't

The valuator gives each archetype a swing (|rNPV_confirmed − rNPV_refuted|) on
the shared stub:

| Archetype | Governing phase | Swing ($M) | Normalized | Openness | VOI score |
|-----------|-----------------|-----------|------------|----------|-----------|
| TARGET_VALIDITY | earliest at-risk (P2) | 368 | 1.00 | 1.0 | **1.00** |
| TOLERABILITY_CEILING | earliest at-risk (P2) | 368 | 1.00 | 0.5 | **0.50** |
| DELIVERY / DOSE | earliest at-risk (P2) | 368 | 1.00 | 1.0 | 1.00 |
| DIFFERENTIATION | pivotal (NDA) | 231 | 0.63 | 1.0 | **0.63** |

- **M1 held at 53.3% but is now *earned*.** The 6 TARGET-decisive programs
  previously beat DIFFERENTIATION only because TARGET is built first (openness
  tie 1.0 = 1.0). Under VOI, TARGET (1.00) genuinely dominates DIFFERENTIATION
  (0.63), so the same 6 hits now reflect value reasoning, not build order. The
  3 DIFFERENTIATION-decisive and 6 TOLERABILITY-decisive programs land exactly
  where they did before, so the headline number is unchanged.
- **M3 rose +13.3%** because those 6 TARGET-vs-DIFFERENTIATION programs no
  longer tie into a spurious abstention — TARGET now clears the 0.15 dominance
  margin (1.00 − 0.63 = 0.37). Net of 5 new near-tie abstentions among
  DIFFERENTIATION-vs-TOLERABILITY pairs (0.63 − 0.50 = 0.13 < 0.15), that is a
  net +2 of 15 correct abstentions.

## Honest caveats — what VOI does *not* fix here

1. **The tolerability ceiling is untouched.** TOLERABILITY_CEILING enters at
   openness 0.5 and shares TARGET's swing (same governing phase), so its VOI is
   capped at 0.50 and every one of the 6 tolerability-decisive programs still
   misses top-1. This is the dominant remaining M1 error source and it is an
   *openness-model* problem (non-severe safety modeled at 0.5), not something a
   branch valuator can rescue.
2. **The governing-index collapse.** `RnpvBranchValuator._governing_index` maps
   every non-DIFFERENTIATION archetype to the same earliest-at-risk phase, so
   they all get the identical swing (368). VOI therefore cannot discriminate
   TARGET vs TOLERABILITY vs DELIVERY vs DOSE by value — it only separates the
   pivotal-phase question (DIFFERENTIATION) from the next-gate pack. With one
   shared stub, VOI reduces to a single global down-weight on DIFFERENTIATION.
3. **Shared-stub sensitivity.** There is no per-program economics in the corpus,
   so all 15 programs are valued against one stub. The *direction* of the M3
   gain (de-tying TARGET-vs-DIFFERENTIATION) is robust, but the exact 66.7% is
   sensitive to the stub's POS ladder: the 5 new abstentions sit only 0.02 inside
   the dominance margin, so different stub economics would flip them. Do not
   quote 66.7% as a calibrated number.

## Where this lands the path

The Step 1.5 mechanism is in place and M1 is now decided by value-at-stake
rather than draft order — the stated goal.

**Correction to an earlier framing (measured 2026-07-03):** the first draft of
this writeup called per-program economics "the real unlock." A follow-up probe
shows the two structural limits are *coupled*, and per-program economics alone is
**inert on this corpus**:

- VOI normalizes swing by the per-program max, so absolute economic magnitude
  (TAM, price) cancels out of the ranking entirely. Only *relative* swings matter.
- The only per-program economic variable that survives normalization is the
  **phase/POS ladder**. But all 15 clean programs are near-uniform in stage
  (13 Phase 3, 2 Phase 2), and rebuilding the 13 Phase-3 programs on a 2-gate
  ladder moves DIFFERENTIATION's normalized VOI only 0.628 → 0.644 — it crosses
  no decision boundary (TARGET still dominates; the DIFF-vs-TOLERABILITY pairs
  still fall 0.14–0.15 inside the abstention margin). M1 and M3 are unchanged.
- Because the governing-index collapse (limit #2) pins every non-DIFFERENTIATION
  archetype to one phase, per-program economics can only ever move the single
  DIFFERENTIATION weight — and that weight is insensitive to the per-program
  phase/magnitude variation this corpus contains.

So the levers that would actually sharpen M1 are, in order:

1. **Corpus breadth** (Step 2 labeling): more archetypes actually decisive
   (esp. DOSE_ADEQUACY / DELIVERY_EXPOSURE, currently zero clean), and
   `single_question_dominant=false` cases for M3. This is domain-labeling
   research and needs sourced, reviewed labels.
2. **Governing-index refinement** in `killer_question.py` so archetypes map to
   distinct phases/POS instead of collapsing onto the earliest gate — a *core*
   engine change, out of harness scope, and only worth doing once the corpus is
   broad enough to reward it.

Per-program economics becomes worthwhile only *after* (2), so it is deferred, not
a near-term Step 2 item.

- **Step 1.5 (this) — DONE:** VOI valuator wired; M1 earned not artifactual; M3 +13.3%.
- **Step 2 (next):** grow the corpus to N≥40 clean **and** capture minimal
  per-program economics so VOI can reason per program, not just re-weight archetypes.
- **Steps 3–8:** approved signatures, M2 replay, empirical LR calibration,
  live-input population, shadow mode, first feature-flagged unlock.
