## Interpretation and limits

The pooled safety abstract is posted for the November 9, 2026 ACR session, still forthcoming on the observation date. Its 47 treated patients are not the SLE registry’s estimated enrollment of 47.
Nine registry matches: seven autoimmune/immune-mediated disease studies (including thyroid-eye disease), one withdrawn IgE-disease study and one completed healthy-volunteer study. See TRIALS.md; no posted results returned. Registration is not outcome verification.
Follow-up closes specific evidence gaps, not discovery recall or autonomous qualification accuracy. Complete current commercial rights remain unverified; the historical cizutamig territory exclusion is documented. Independent BD usefulness and analyst time remain unmeasured.
