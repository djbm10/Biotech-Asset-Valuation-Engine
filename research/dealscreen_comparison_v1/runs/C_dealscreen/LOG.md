## What the tool got wrong or could not do
1. **Ontology:** "Claudin 18.2" did not resolve, so the default run gives 0 eligible. That is useless without a manual synonym map.
2. **Offline research crash:** the offline provider crashes on partial dates (YYYY-MM or YYYY) in the protocol-supplied evidence library.
3. **Over-trusting landscape text:**
   - **Modality:** it trusts the landscape's modality text. LCB02A is labelled "Monoclonal antibody" but is an ADC, so it was wrongly EXCLUDED. IBI343 is labelled "Imaging agent" but is an ADC, so it went to UNRESOLVED rather than IN.
   - **Imaging agents:** CL-61 and CL-62 are not excluded; their modality stays UNKNOWN.
   - **Cell therapy and fusion protein:** TAC01 (a T-cell therapy) and the LB4330 IL-10 fusion protein are not excluded because their modality is blank.
4. **Indication:** the indication gate is UNKNOWN for 28 programs, including clear gastric Ph3 ADCs (LM-302, ATG-022). The agent has no INDICATION question, so this cannot clear without a human confirmation.
5. **Missed identity:** it missed EO-3021 = SYSA1801 (CL-14/CL-15), even though the evidence says "EO-3021 (SYSA1801)", and the possible ANO31905/QLS31905 link. It also missed SKB315 = MK-1200 (Merck) as a rights fact.
6. **Rights vocabulary:** it has no way to express "rights returned" or "option held by another company" as a rights state.
7. **Agent output:** the agent's `us_rights` findings put a whole sentence in the value field, with no structured holder or territory.
8. **Valuation:** the prototype CLI printed out-of-order bear/base/bull license scenarios without explaining them.
9. **`market_status`:** null for every row in the screening JSON, including marketed zolbetuximab.
