# Autoimmune T-cell engager leads: bounded ACR/company discovery

**UNVALIDATED research only. Do not use for trade sizing or acquisition probability.**
Search date: 24 October 2026 UTC. Independent final BD review: **OUTSTANDING**.

**Result:** one previously missing named programme met the frozen discovery endpoint:
**HXN-1031**. It is a **preclinical research lead**, not a clinically qualified or available
licensing opportunity. Cizutamig and CDR111 were also newly nominated relative to the old
baseline, but do not receive endpoint credit because the acquired pages do not explicitly
establish their antibody format. No scientific gates or validation grades were changed.

The question was: which CD19- **or** BCMA-directed T-cell engager antibody programmes
are being developed for autoimmune disease? Four fixed generic queries contained no asset
or company names. No reference URLs, candidate lists or authority-derived asset seeds were
supplied to acquisition. This is independent of name seeding, but uses an exposed development
reference and manual evidence adjudication; it is not a blind holdout or a recall benchmark.

**HXN-1031 — the formal discovery success.** Generic query Q1 returned an abstract whose
URL does not contain the candidate name (rank 9). The unchanged extractor nominated the
name from the fetched page. The abstract explicitly connects the named compound, its
trispecific antibody design, targets and autoimmune intent. Its experiments include tumour
models and B-cell/plasma-cell depletion; they do not demonstrate benefit in autoimmune
patients. The source's publication metadata is 1 September 2025. Helixon is an author
affiliation, not a verified rights holder. [Primary ACR abstract](https://acrabstracts.org/abstract/development-of-a-noval-111-cd19-and-bcma-dual-targeted-t-cell-engager-for-autoimmune-diseases/).

**Cizutamig — the most immediate clinical follow-up in this small set.** Q1 rank 3 reports
47 treated patients across several autoimmune conditions in investigational studies and named
patient use. This is a heterogeneous safety report, not controlled efficacy evidence or a
validated human-PoC determination. Its metadata says 1 September 2026; the associated
November 2026 meeting has not yet occurred. Confirm the antibody scaffold and underlying
trial-level evidence before diligence. Candid/UCB disclosures do not settle current rights.
[Primary ACR abstract](https://acrabstracts.org/abstract/pooled-safety-of-cizutamig-a-bcmaxcd3-t-cell-engager-in-patients-with-autoimmune-diseases/).

**CDR111 — earlier-stage follow-up.** Q1 rank 10 reports dual-target depletion in cell assays,
ex-vivo samples and mice. Patient-derived samples must not be presented as clinical responses.
The page calls the product a T-cell engager but leaves its precise antibody format unverified
under this experiment's strict criterion. [Primary ACR abstract](https://acrabstracts.org/abstract/cdr111-is-a-novel-cd19-and-bcma-dual-targeting-t-cell-engager-tce-for-the-treatment-of-severe-and-refractory-autoimmune-diseases/).

**Company-source contribution:** Kali's issuer release reports first dosing of **KT501** in
Phase 1a rheumatoid arthritis and explicitly describes its trispecific antibody format.
This provides direct development evidence, not efficacy. KT501 was already nominated in
A_base, so it earns **no new-discovery credit**. Registry confirmation and rights remain open.
[Issuer release](https://www.kalitherapeutics.com/post/kali-therapeutics-announces-first-patient-dosed-in-phase1a-clinical-trial-of-kt501-for-rheumatoid-ar).

## What the experiment actually established

70 search results → 18 selected pages → 17 fetched/indexed pages → 643 literal nominations
→ 306 normalized strings. Four programmes received manual investigational-asset identification;
only one met every primary endpoint requirement. **Scientific eligibility was not evaluated;
zero BD opportunities have confirmed availability.** Blinatumomab/teclistamab remained
comparators; wrong-format GC012F and wrong-target/format IMC-002 were not promoted.

Acquisition and nomination took 18 seconds and peaked at 76 MiB RSS, excluding search and
manual review. One issuer page failed and was not replaced.
The nomination layer still emits substantial navigation/prose noise. This is a source-recovery
improvement, not evidence that the complete engine produces a trustworthy shortlist.

The old stored baseline did not nominate HXN-1031, CDR111 or cizutamig. Today's search ranking
and retrieved pages cannot reconstruct historical retrieval: no causal recall percentage or
sealed-experiment comparison is claimed. LBL-051, A-319 and gamgertamig remain missing here.
Other new names outside the frozen reference receive no retrospective endpoint credit.
