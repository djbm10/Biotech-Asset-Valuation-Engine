## How to read evidence status

- **SUPPORTED:** a dated assertion supported in the previous manual primary-source review. It is
  not independent BD verification, validated engine qualification, or a warranty of source accuracy.
- **UNVERIFIED:** a claim/observation requiring further interpretation or corroboration. Cizutamig's
  pooled safety observations do not establish indication-specific efficacy or human PoC.
- **UNKNOWN:** not established in this review. Absence of a trial/deal in a bounded search is not
  proof that none exists. Historic territory disclosures do not establish complete current rights.
- **CONTRADICTORY:** unresolved incompatible evidence; do not select the more favorable assertion.

Company sponsorship, author affiliation, pipeline listing, acquisition, territorial rights and
partnering availability answer different questions. In particular, UCB's announced completed Candid
acquisition makes cizutamig an acquired program; it does not prove global rights or an open deal.
HXN-1031 remains a preclinical research lead, with clinical entry and legal rights unknown.
