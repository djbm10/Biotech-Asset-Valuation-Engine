## Fixed inputs and bounds

2024-12-06 disclosure (URL in existing evidence ledger). No historical code or NCT identifier is
provided to runtime as a seed. Registry intervention otherNames may generate one-hop retrieval
leads; explicit code-parenthetical pairs in company text may extend them. Every edge must retain

edges. Multiple otherNames, combination/generic labels and conflicting pair relationships require
review and cannot silently drive expansion. Parenthetical pairs are retrieval leads, never proof

Limits: 6 distinct query terms, 3 discovery rounds, 2 pages/term, pageSize 100, 2 MiB per response,
10 company documents, 2 MiB/document. Sequential calls, no recursion beyond budget. Failed, malformed,
truncated and successful-zero searches remain distinct. Incomplete alias vocabulary always means
asset-history completeness UNKNOWN, even if all pages for all issued terms are returned.
Do not filter by indication, phase, recruiting status or positive outcome. Retain terminated,
withdrawn, oncology and healthy-volunteer records. A registry hit alone is not identity confirmation.
Retain exact intervention matching evidence and query provenance separately from possible related hits.
No NCT-specific branch, product-specific regex or predefined historical-code table.

query injection, partial-name match and conflicting duplicate record. These must not produce an
automatic identity merge; ambiguity is quarantined, errors never become complete-zero evidence.

and focused tests precede final full tests/se regression and Ruff.
